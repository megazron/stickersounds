Stickers that talk

Chat stickers are silent, and half the fun of a meme is the sound. StickerSounds is a demo of one idea: a sticker that carries its own sound.

A sound sticker is just the normal sticker image (512×512 WebP) plus a short audio clip (a few seconds). Send it and it plays once for everyone in the chat; tap it to play it again. Turn auto-play off with the speaker button.

The built-in packs are original drawings with the famous lines spoken by an offline voice and mixed with sound effects. Use + Make to turn any meme picture and clip on your phone into a sound sticker.

This is an independent demo. It is not affiliated with any messaging app.
